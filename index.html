About

The structure of data,the logic of valuation.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Zhejiang University and part of a joint doctoral training program at the Shenzhen Loop Area Institute.

I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Zhejiang University in 2024. My work combines rigorous mathematical analysis with practical algorithm design, focusing on how to value data fairly and estimate contribution efficiently at scale.

Research

What I work on

I study principled and scalable ways to understand the value, ownership, and contribution of data in machine learning systems.

01

Data market & data valuation

Studying how data is priced and exchanged while fairly quantifying the contributions of data points, features, and participants.

02

Shapley-like estimation & adaptive sampling

Designing efficient estimators and allocating sampling budgets adaptively to reduce utility evaluations while preserving theoretical guarantees.

03

Cooperative game theory

Developing principled notions of value and fairness for cooperative settings, with an emphasis on Shapley-like solution concepts.

04

Trustworthy data systems

Protecting dataset ownership and utility through robust, machine-learning-aware watermarking.
